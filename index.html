Our story

Brewed with
memory &
meaning.

What started as a tiny stall at the Tarlac Public Market has grown into a beloved café — but we've never forgotten where we came from, or who taught us how to make the perfect cup.

Est. 2009
TARLAC CITY
The beginning

From Lola's
Kitchen

In 2009, Lola Remedios Cruz began selling brewed coffee and fresh pandesal from her home near Tarlac's public market. Word spread — not through ads, but through neighbors telling neighbors about the cup that tasted like Sunday mornings.

By 2012, we opened our first proper café along MacArthur Highway. Lola Remy still visits every Saturday to check on the beans.

Our craft

The Central
Luzon Difference

We source from smallhold farms we've known for over a decade. The rich lowland soil of Tarlac produces bold, full-bodied brews that carry the warmth of the region in every sip.

We roast in small batches three times a week to ensure every cup is as fresh as possible.

What we believe

Our Values

🌿

Locally Rooted

Every bean, every egg, every loaf comes from within the Tarlac region. We support the farmers who feed our community.
